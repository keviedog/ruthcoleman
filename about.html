About Me

Hey, I'm Ruth , a Creative Digital Media student who loves making work that's equal parts thoughtful and beautiful. I've had the chance to bring one real brand to life: I designed the logo, posters and full Instagram identity for a yoga business, working with the owner to shape how the brand should feel from the ground up. Alongside that, I've mapped the user flows for a pet adoption app and animated scenes for a group short episode in Blender. I'm a very visual person. I notice the details, I like things neat, and I care about how a design makes people feel. I'm organised, I work well in a team, and I'm happy stepping up to lead a project when it needs direction. I'm looking for a design role where I can keep making user-focused work — the kind that's not just nice to look at, but actually works for the people using it.
