Design & communications – college brochure · 2025

Creative Digital Media Brochure

A four-page brochure for TU Dublin's Creative Digital Media programme, designed to give prospective students a clear picture of what the course actually involves. The brief asked for something that felt creative rather than institutional — the challenge was balancing personality with the practical information a school brochure needs..

  • Tools Illustrator (illustration), InDesign (layout)
  • Format Four-page brochure
  • Role Concept, illustration, layout

The project followed the double diamond: Discover, Define, Develop, Deliver. Each stage was documented across a series of artboards, from broad visual research through to the final artwork.

01

Discover

Collected imagery across illustration, collage, photography and print, building a visual vocabulary before committing to a direction.

02

Define

Annotated the research to pull out recurring themes: surreal portraiture, botanical line work, collage, and a bold retro palette.

03

Develop

Moved from thumbnail sketches into refinement. Early directions explored photographic collage and botanical illustration before the robot head emerged as the strongest, most distinctive route.

04

Deliver

Resolved the final artwork, palette and layout, then applied it across the cover and inside spread.

designdesign designdesign designdesign designdesign
05

Cover

The cover carries the three title words inside a pink cloud that grows out of the robot's exposed brain, sitting against a teal diagonal swirl. The composition is deliberately top-heavy so the wordmark reads first and the illustration holds the space below.

06

Type

Three treatments in play: a script for "Creative", a pixel face for "Digital", and a heavy sans for "Media". Each word signals a different discipline within the course, which is the point of the title.

07

Palette

Built from the colour studies: deep teal as the ground, coral pink as the accent, with mint, lilac and warm neutrals for the illustration. Teal and pink do the structural work; the rest adds depth without competing.

08

Inside spread

Year-by-year breakdowns sit in pink cloud shapes that echo the cover, with modules and course information set in a clean grid. The clouds keep the inside feeling consistent with the front while staying readable.

A last-minute cover change

The cover art was revised at the final stage. The earlier sketch used a photographic collage, which competed with the type and lost the illustration at small sizes. The final version isolates the robot head against the teal swirl so the wordmark reads first, and the pink cloud holds the three title words as a single block.

Outcome

A finished four-page brochure that carries the programme's personality without losing the information. The process record shows a clear line from research through to final artwork, including a late revision made on the strength of how the cover performed at print size.
